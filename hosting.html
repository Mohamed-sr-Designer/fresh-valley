The problem

We've all brought the forgettable gift.

We reach for them because they're easy — not because they're meant. A real gift should say I thought about you. Most just say I stopped on the way.

  • Chocolates.— melt by the door
  • Flowers.— wilt by morning
  • A bottle of cola.— no one opens
  • The usual sweets.— everyone forgets

Our philosophy

Bring the season. Bring something alive.

Fruit at its peak is honest — it can't be faked or rushed. When you bring the season, you bring something that was perfect for exactly this moment. That isn't a gift you grabbed. It's a gift you timed.
