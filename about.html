Our story

Produce, treated with the care it deserves.

Fresh Valley is a small produce house. We work directly with Egypt's finest growers — the same farms that supply Europe's best tables — and keep the best of their harvest here, for homes in Cairo.

Browse the harvest

Where it began

We wanted to keep some of the best here.

We started Fresh Valley with a simple frustration: the best of Egypt's produce was leaving the country, while the rest filled the shelves at home.

So we went to the fields ourselves. We choose that produce at first light, then shape it into boxes and collections for the way people host today: generously, beautifully, and without a fuss. An everyday purchase, raised to the standard of an occasion.

We are based in Cairo, and we deliver to the neighbourhoods we know best — carefully, on time, and in packaging worth keeping.

Behind the quality

Graded like an export house.

Nothing reaches your table by accident. Every piece of produce passes through the same four steps the export market demands.

  1. 01

    Selection

    We begin with Egypt's best growers, and only the lots that meet export specification. Most of what fills an ordinary market never makes the cut.

  2. 02

    Grading

    Each piece is sorted by hand for size, colour, firmness and ripeness. The rest is set aside — what reaches you has earned its place.

  3. 03

    Cold chain

    Produce is cooled within hours of harvest and kept at the right temperature, all the way to your door. Freshness is a logistics problem, and we treat it like one.

  4. 04

    Presentation

    Finally, we pack to arrive looking like a gift — because that is increasingly how good produce is used.
