Common questions

Still wondering? Message us on WhatsApp — it's the fastest way to a real person.

When will my order arrive?

Choose a next-day delivery slot at checkout — morning, midday, afternoon or evening. We'll send an update by SMS when your produce is on the way.

What does 'export-grade' mean?

It is the standard the export market demands: each piece graded by hand for size, colour, firmness and ripeness. Anything that doesn't pass is set aside, so what reaches you is consistent and beautiful.

What if something isn't perfect?

Then it isn't on us to keep. Tell us within 24 hours and we'll replace it or refund it — no debate. Quality is the whole promise.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Yes. Any box can be set to a weekly or fortnightly schedule from your account, and you can pause or change it any time.

How is delivery priced?

Complimentary on orders over EGP 600, and a flat EGP 45 below that, across all our areas.
